# Copper + Cloves — Premium Wellness Cafe Ecosystem

## Vision
Copper + Cloves (C+C) is a premium chain of 3 plant-based wellness cafes in Bangalore, combining hyper-local meal subscriptions, artisanal retail products, and community sanctuary spaces. The brand ecosystem website serves as the digital front door to this holistic wellness movement — where neighborhood convenience meets intentional nutrition.

Target: Bangalore's health-conscious professionals, fitness enthusiasts, and mindful eaters seeking premium plant-based nourishment without compromise.

## Design
**Direction:** High-end wellness retreat meets editorial food magazine — sun-drenched sanctuary spaces with warm, analog-film quality.

**Palette:**
- `--background: 30 35% 96% (warm sand/cream #FAF7F2)` — dominant canvas
- `--foreground: 20 15% 18% (deep charcoal #2C2520)` — primary text
- `--primary: 18 55% 55% (terracotta/copper #C87350)` — earned accent for CTAs
- `--secondary: 150 18% 42% (forest green #597566)` — wellness signals
- `--accent: 18 55% 55% (copper, same as primary)` — key interactive moments
- `--muted: 30 25% 88% (warm grey #E8E2DA)` — subtle backgrounds
- `--card: 30 40% 98% (lighter cream #FCFAF7)` — elevated surfaces
- `--border: 30 20% 85% (soft warm border #DED7CC)` — gentle separators

**Typography:**
- Display (headlines): Newsreader (serif, 600/700 weights)
- Body: Lora (serif, 400/600 weights)
- System fallbacks: Georgia, serif

**Style Direction:** Generous whitespace, soft rounded corners (rounded-lg/xl), warm shadows, subtle hover elevations, sun-drenched photography with the palette's warm tones. Editorial layout rhythm with asymmetric compositions on feature sections.

## Features
**Core Pages:**
1. **Landing/Home:** Hero + brand philosophy (3 pillars) + bestseller retail grid + community events teaser
2. **Our Outlets:** 3-card grid showcasing Bangalore cafe locations with amenities, hours, delivery radius
3. **Meal Subscription:** Value prop + how-it-works sequence + plan configurator with pincode checker
4. **Blog (The Notebook):** Featured article hero + filterable grid (Recipes, Nutrition, Lifestyle, Community)
5. **Careers:** Culture statement + benefits grid + current openings accordion
6. **Contact/Enquiries:** Split-screen with contact info + inquiry form

**Global Components:**
- Sticky header: Logo + nav links + "Subscribe Now" CTA
- Footer: Brand statement + quick links + locations + newsletter capture
